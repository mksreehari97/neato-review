SERVICE DETAILS

Running a business is already a full-time job. Between meetings, deadlines, customers, employees, and everything in between, keeping your workplace consistently clean can easily fall to the bottom of the list. But a clean workplace isn’t just about appearances—it creates a healthier, more welcoming, and more productive environment for everyone who walks through your doors.

That’s where NeatO’s Commercial Cleaning comes in.

Our commercial cleaning service provides a thorough, professional clean designed around the needs of your workplace. Whether you manage an office, retail space, professional facility, or commercial property, we take care of the cleaning so you can focus on what matters most—running your business.

From high-touch surfaces and floors to kitchens, washrooms, common areas, and workspaces, NeatO helps keep your commercial space looking fresh, organized, and ready for business.

A cleaner workplace. A better first impression. A healthier environment.

And the best part? You don’t have to worry about fitting cleaning into your already busy schedule.

Click. Tap. Clean in a Snap.

What’s Included in NeatO’s Standard Clean?

OFFICES & WORKSPACES

  • Clean and disinfect desks, workstations, and accessible surfaces
  • Dust and wipe shelves, cabinets, tables, and furniture
  • Clean and disinfect high-touch areas, including door handles and switches
  • Dust reachable light fixtures and ceiling fans
  • Clean interior glass doors and accessible glass surfaces
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Deep vacuum and mop hard floors
  • Dust and wipe baseboards
  • Remove visible cobwebs
  • Empty trash and replace trash bags

KITCHENS & BREAK ROOMS

  • Clean and disinfect countertops and preparation areas
  • Clean sinks and faucets
  • Wipe down exterior surfaces of appliances
  • Clean tables, chairs, and dining areas
  • Clean and disinfect high-touch surfaces
  • Empty trash
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Empty trash and replace trash bags

RESTROOMS

  • Scrub and disinfect toilets, sinks, and fixtures
  • Clean and disinfect countertops
  • Clean mirrors
  • Wipe down exterior of cabinets
  • Vacuum/Mopping of bathroom floors
  • Clean exterior cabinet surfaces
  • Replenish client-provided restroom supplies

COMMON AREAS & ENTRANCES

  • Vacuum carpets and entrance mats
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Dust and wipe tables, chairs, and accessible surfaces
  • Clean interior entry doors and glass
  • Dust reachable surfaces and fixture
  • Remove visible cobwebs
  • Empty trash and recycling bins

What’s NOT Included in NeatO’s Standard Clean?

  • Interior window cleaning unless added as an extra service
  • Interior blinds unless added as an extra service
  • Carpet washing or extraction
  • High or difficult-to-reach areas
  • Exterior window cleaning
  • Deep cleaning inside appliances
  • Moving heavy furniture or equipment
  • Specialized restoration services

What NeatO does not Clean

  • Biohazardous or hazardous materials
  • Mold remediation
  • Rust removal
  • Pest or insect infestations
  • Construction debris requiring specialized removal
  • Areas requiring specialized industrial cleaning equipment
  • Extremely high ceilings, windows, or fixtures without appropriate access equipment
