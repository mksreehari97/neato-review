SERVICE DETAILS

Let’s be honest. It’s easy to forget that cleaning your own home is self care. It definitely doesn’t feel like it until you’re done cleaning and at that point you are in a full flop sweat.

So it gets brushed aside until next weekend. And the next weekend. All of a sudden it’s been months and that blanket of dust is back, staring at you on the couch, which is probably full of crumbs and pet hair. What to do?!

NeatO’s Deep Clean is a thorough top to bottom clean that feels like you hit the reset button on your home. The Deep Clean is a must for anyone who hasn’t had a proper cleaning in over 3 months. The amount of accumulated dust, spider webs, dirt, and grime within just a few months can be surprising. We all know what it’s like to lie in bed and stare at the tidal wave of dust hanging on the edge of the fan blade. It’s not pretty. So do yourself a favor and book your deep clean online. It’s the least talked about form of self-care! Future you will thank you!

Does the inside of your fridge need a deep clean as well? Has your oven taken a beating and could use a reset? No problem! Easily customize your apartment cleaning or home cleaning by adding some extra services on the “Extras” page during your online booking. It’s as easy as…

Click. Tap. Clean in a Snap.

What’s Included in NeatO’s Standard Clean?

KITCHEN

  • Clean and disinfect all counters, appliances, & stovetop
  • Clean interior of microwave
  • Scrub of kitchen sink
  • Wipe down exterior of refrigerator (make sure to remove all your cute magnets if you want us to get under)
  • Wipe down exterior of cabinets and drawers
  • Clean all light fixtures, switches, handles, and knobs
  • MVacuum/Mop kitchen floors
  • Polish all stainless steel
  • Empty trash and replace trash bags

BEDROOMS & COMMON SPACES

  • Move furniture (when possible), vacuum and clean under and behind.
  • Dust and wipe ceiling fans (reachable with step stool)
  • Detailed dusting and wiping all surfaces ( including dressers, nightstands, shelves, etc.) and visible belongings
  • Make the bed (We will change sheets if clean sheets are placed on bed)
  • Deep Vacuum of sofa/couch
  • Deep Vacuum/Steam Mop all floors
  • Clean mirrors and glass surfaces
  • Dusting baseboards
  • Empty trash
  • Wipe switch plates, switches, and door knobs
  • Dust and Wipe baseboards
  • Remove all visible cobwebs

BATHROOMS

  • Scrub and disinfect sinks, toilets, and showers/tubs
  • Clean light fixtures, switches, handles, and knobs
  • VClean mirrors
  • Wipe down exterior of cabinets
  • Vacuum/Mopping of bathroom floors
  • Dust baseboards
  • Dust window sills

What’s NOT Included in NeatO’s Standard Clean?

  • Deep Cleaning of Home
  • Interior Blinds
  • Inside Fridge
  • Inside Oven
  • Inside Drawers/Cabinets
  • Interior windows, window tracks, and sills

What NeatO does not Clean

  • Interior Windows
  • Inside Washer/Dryer
  • Extremely high shelves/windows
  • Carpet Washing
  • Rust/Mold Removal
